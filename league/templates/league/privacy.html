Privacy Policy

Last updated July 23, 2026

Information collected

QueueUp stores account information such as your display name, username, email address, password hash, approval status, optional profile image, submissions, votes, achievements, and league activity. If you enable notifications, it also stores a browser push-subscription endpoint and cryptographic keys for that device.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the league, approve accounts, keep submissions anonymous until reveal, calculate scores and achievements, send requested notifications, prevent abuse, troubleshoot the service, and maintain security.

Spotify data

Song searches and submissions use Spotify metadata such as track title, artist, album, artwork, preview URL, explicit-content flag, and Spotify identifiers. An administrator may connect a Spotify account for playlist creation. QueueUp does not receive your Spotify password.

Sharing

League activity is shared with other approved members as needed for gameplay. Submissions remain hidden from profile history until their round is revealed. Data is not sold. It may be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the service, or to vendors needed to host and operate QueueUp.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while needed to operate the league and preserve its history. Contact league staff to request correction or deletion. Some records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, backups, legal obligations, or league integrity.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online system can guarantee complete security. Use a unique password and promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

Children

QueueUp is intended for use only with the permission and supervision appropriate for the league's participants. The operator is responsible for determining whether parental consent or additional notices are required.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Questions or privacy requests should be directed to the staff member who operates your QueueUp league.

This starter policy is general information, not legal advice. The league operator should review it for the laws and participants that apply.
